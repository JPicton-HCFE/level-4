# ITSM Plan Notes
These are some notes for the IT service Management Plan we'll be making for an assignment.

What is an ITSM?
Why is it important?


  - Service Desk: The central point of contact for users to report issues or request services.
    - Things to keep track of: Time, Date, Name (Person who reported it), Fault Description, Location, Device/App, Allocated Technician (Not needed).
  - Incident Management: Restoring normal service operations as quickly as possible, such as fixing a broken laptop.
  - Problem Management: Identify the root cause of reucrring incidents to prevent future issues.
    - Something to take note, could create a Routine Maintanence plan, for example replacing the paper in the printer once a day etc.
    - Another one would be to remove user accounts that no longer are needed in the company etc.
  - Change Management: Managing IT infrastructure changes to minimise disruptions.
  - Service Request Management: Handling user requests for new services (e.g. software install)
    - Research Software Aquasition policy, or AUP (Acceptable Use Policy). Something else to note is where to put these, for example just an open policy or within employment contract.
  - Focus on People & Process: Ensure staff are trained and processess are documented and followed.
