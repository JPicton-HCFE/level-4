What is Cyber Security?

Let's begin with what Cyber Security is, Cyber Security is the prevention of cyber attacks, ensuring procedures are put in place to reduce the effects of an attack or full prevention of an attack. Whilst at it's core Cyber Security's function is to defend against potential or ongoing online threats.

It's the protection of vast amounts of data which can be public or private information either stored locally (in house/internal) or externally via the cloud. It's the practice used to protect people, systems, and data by using or implementing various techbologies, processes and policies.

Why is it Important?

Ensuring that the principles of Cyber Security are enforced and used effectively prevent users, businesses and governments from losing their data, access to devices, or denied service. These attacks can disrupt, damage and destroy businesses, communities and lives.

With each connected device to a network being a PAN(Personal Area Network), LAN(Local Area Network), or WAN(Wide Area Network) that is another point for an attack, even things like light bulbs that connect to the internet now are susceptible to attacks. This can also include things like the washing machine, printer, fridge, smart tv etc.

As the increase of our daily lives continues to include more "smart" solutions to simple appliances, the area of attack widens. With the feature of convience also comes the cost of privacy and security. For a better understanding how this can be done here is an article by Cyber Security News which highlights how each newly installed and setup device(s) with access to Wi-Fi, even those light-bulbs, can be used as a cyber weapon against your network.
